## Creator Studio release reconciliation

Areas **12 (Media Studio)**, **16 (Video & livestreaming)**, and **18 (Events & timelines)** have scoped Creator evidence for private media, native video delivery, and protected Theater recovery. Whole-area grades are held. Native live, recording, and paid delivery remain beta for supervised use, with third-party rollout disabled pending E3. The release notes identify the candidate, provider boundaries, and open verification gates. [Creator release notes](../creator-studio/CRE-16-RELEASE-NOTES.md)

**Method — the no-relabeling rule, applied**

- E-levels follow the governing plan's ladder literally (E0
  concept/stub, E1 mounted useful action with persisted readback or
  verifiable output, E2 connected workflow with scoped permissions and
  a proven negative case, E3 operationally supported, E4 mature).
- **No level was upgraded on inference.** Exactly one upward regrade
  was made in this pass — #5 Complete SEO, E1 → E2 — on executed proof
  that post-dates the RC2 audit (SEO 1.0 pass, PR #95, live probe
  43/43; the same pass closed DEBT-14). Every other row keeps its RC2
  level; where post-audit work materially advanced a row's evidence
  without completing its deciding proof (#10, #15), the row is
  annotated and marked stale instead of regraded.
- Split rows keep their parts: #16 (video E1 / livestreams E0), #59
  (SEO-suggestion path E2 / wider catalog E1), #74 (REST E2 / GraphQL
  NOT VERIFIED). Per the RC3 contract, the weakest required
  sub-capability is the row's floor; the CSV carries both the display
  level and the floor.
- Provider status is recorded independently of E-level (sandbox,
  harness-only, test-mode, unconfigured, held). A provider-blocked
  workflow keeps that status until it is measured.
- Owning passes use the RC3 pass numbering adopted Nov 20, 2026
  (00/00A baseline, 01–07 Wave 1, 08–16 Wave 2, 17–23 Wave 3, 24–25
  freeze/exit). Every one of the 90 rows has a primary owning pass;
  passes 00, 00A, 24 and 25 own the whole ledger rather than single
  rows and are not repeated per row.
