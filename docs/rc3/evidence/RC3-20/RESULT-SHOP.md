- `src/app/(frontend)/checkout/page.tsx` — renders `CartView mode="checkout"`.
- `src/modules/commerce/CheckoutStatus.tsx` — formatted money (was raw minor units), buyer-language payment states, history separator fix.
- `src/app/(frontend)/store/page.tsx` — search + server pagination; 100-product ceiling removed.
- `tests/integration/rc320-shop-journey.integration.test.ts` — NEW: the 3-test definition-of-done proof (consent fidelity, full guest journey, discovery data path).
- `tests/rc1-11c/journey.ts`, `tests/rc1-11d/journey.ts`, `tests/rc1-11e/journey.ts`, `tests/rc1-17e/payment-journey.ts` — selectors follow the mandated copy change ("Review your order"; terms checkbox by name, required once a second checkbox exists).
