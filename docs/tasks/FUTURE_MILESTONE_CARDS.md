# Future milestone cards (M05–M15)

These are bounded planning records. `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` Prompts 5–15 remain the execution source of truth. Every card is planned; no scope below is evidence of implementation.

## M05 — Public presentation and discovery

**M05-A:** prove theme-independent rendering for publications and the roadmap's profiles, portfolios, forums, feeds, navigation, SEO, structured data, and privacy-safe discovery boundaries. Entry: accepted M04. Excludes visual composition and later capability families.

## M06 — Visual composition

**M06-A:** prove a versioned guided section/block composition boundary for finished sites and expressive personal Spaces. Saved JSX, HTML, screenshots, and builder-internal state are not durable content. Entry: accepted M05.

## M07 — Passwordless members, community, and security

**M07-A:** prove canonical Member identity and Space access, including pinned Reown/SIWX verification where enabled, while retaining the separation between wallet connection, authentication, and payment. Profiles, relationships, encrypted/burnable messages, forums, moderation, and abuse controls are later M07 gates. Entry: accepted M06.

## M08 — Extensions and provider lifecycle

**M08-A:** prove a generic module/provider manifest and discovery path with compatibility, connections, secrets, disable, replacement, upgrade, export, and failure isolation. Provider claims use the plan's live/sandbox/contract/scaffolded/deferred statuses. Entry: accepted M07.

## M09 — Bring-your-own AI

**M09-A:** establish the optional provider-neutral task-routing, context, approval, audit, privacy, and failure-isolation boundary. AI may assist but cannot acquire publishing or operational authority. Entry: accepted M08.

## M10 — Media production

**M10-A:** prove portable media ingestion, ownership, derivatives, publishing, and accessibility boundaries for books, audio, video, interviews, livestreams, transcription, and TTS. Production adapter workflows are later M10 gates. Entry: accepted M09.

## M11 — Social command center and calendar

**M11-A:** prove personal, Space, and publication calendar ownership with audited campaign/scheduling contracts; Events remain distinct from planning entries as preserved by M03.5. Network execution, graphic studio, and campaign proof are later gates. Entry: accepted M10.

## M12 — Audience and email

**M12-A:** prove forms, subscriber consent, notification preferences, and provider-isolated audience ownership before delivery automation. Newsletter composition, scheduling, delivery, and recipient proof are later M12 gates. Entry: accepted M11.

## M13 — Global creator commerce

**M13-A:** prove Space-owned merchant/provider, payment-method eligibility, cart/order, donation/subscription, crowdfunding, crypto-payment, and POD contract boundaries without custody or hard-coded rails. Local-method discovery and creator fulfilment are later M13 gates. Entry: accepted M12.

## M14 — Analytics and reporting

**M14-A:** prove first-party measurement, consent, identity boundaries, and deduplicated attribution inputs before dashboards or recommendations. Goals, exceptions, reporting, and journey attribution are later M14 gates. Entry: accepted M13.

## M15 — Portability, hardening, and launch

**M15-A:** prove versioned, idempotent import/export ownership, manifests, non-secret handling, migration fidelity reporting, and a disposable round-trip boundary. Hardening, release tiers, Renegade Party configuration/theme proof, clean-host transfer, and launch rehearsal are later gates. Entry: accepted M14.
