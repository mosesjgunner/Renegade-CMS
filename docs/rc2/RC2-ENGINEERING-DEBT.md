Divergences a merge must resolve: profile `handle` is globally unique with `handleChangedAt`/`handleHistory` tracking; space `handle` is unique only per `(site, handle)` (composite index) and the space record carries `canonicalPath` defaulting to `/members/` — the space literally encodes the profile's URL space. Space-only fields: site, profile (relation), canonicalPath, capabilityFields(), providerOwnership, moderationState, suspendedAt, transferToMember, transferState.

Also holding space relations: `src/collections/canonical-shared.ts:204` (scope fields), `src/collections/Collaboration.ts:12`, `src/collections/Integrations.ts:12`; enumerated in `src/modules/portability/workflow.ts:15` and `src/modules/operations/onboarding.ts:238`.
