## Staff role source of truth

`src/collections/Users.ts` stores only `owner`, `administrator`, and `staff` on authenticated staff accounts. Editorial roles such as `editor` and `publisher`, community `moderator`, and commerce operator labels are used by member/workflow domains; they are not provisioned staff login roles in this checkout. `src/modules/admin/access-policy.ts` is the frontend shell policy.

## Role model decision

Keep the authenticated admin role set at `owner`, `administrator`, and site-assigned `staff`. Do not add `publisher`, `editor`, `moderator`, or `commerce` as login roles in this pass: the product has no complete capability matrix for those personas, and adding role labels without route/action enforcement would grant misleading access. The Users role field now explains this. In workflow actions, owner/administrator map to publisher and staff maps to editor; community moderation is an admin capability rather than a separate moderator account. The supported restricted account is staff limited by `adminSites`; it currently has the site’s supported operator sections rather than a separate per-feature permission set. Tests verify unsupported persona values are denied at the admin shell and representative protected routes.

## Route and entity boundaries

- Frontend admin layout rejects anonymous and non-staff roles. Sensitive frontend route pages retain their own role checks; owner telemetry remains owner-only.

Owner-only Analytics is hidden from administrator/staff. Maintenance is hidden from staff; Capability Center is owner-only and Legacy Migration allows owner/administrator.

Staff community-member reads are limited to members with a membership at an assigned site, while identity-link writes are service-only. Staff role assignment beyond the three stored login roles is not implemented; the menu does not claim that specialized editor, moderator, or commerce staff roles can be administered.
- Telemetry page and dashboard/funnel/experiment APIs require owner, matching the visible Analytics section.

- Owner-managed `users.adminSites` grants scope site-bearing collections and the reviewed custom admin routes. Staff need an assigned `siteId` for site operations; Dashboard, Commerce, Fulfillment, Intelligence, Social, and Audience require the active site before loading staff views. Server route queries use the authenticated user and selected site. Route checks also resolve the actual entity site before privileged writes for workflow, scheduled publishing, commerce, discovery, redirects, AI, social, integrations, and audience operations. Scheduled publish jobs are scoped through their article relation because the job record has no direct site field. Related Social, Audience, Commerce, Identity, Integration, Network, and Publishing records resolve staff scope through the parent site-bearing record; service-owned credential, token, notification, and audit mutations remain blocked from the admin API. Cross-site provider counts are suppressed for staff. The separate migration `20260925_000000_admin_site_grants` is pending in the configured database, so database-backed staff access is not yet deployable there.
- Role coverage is limited to authenticated staff roles `owner`, `administrator`, and `staff`. Publisher/editor, moderator, and commerce operator are member/workflow concepts, not independent staff login roles. Workflow actions map staff to editor capabilities; template editing is administrator/owner only and is hidden for staff. Fresh migration execution was verified against disposable `admin00_20260925_release_acceptance`; live database-backed site access and browser session tests remain outstanding.
- Provider inventory combines site-owned Social, Merchant, POD, AI, API-client, and webhook records with runtime capability rows for SMTP/local mail capture, Twilio/local telecom emulator, local or S3 media storage, ActivityPub, Stripe test checkout, and the unimplemented external video adapter. It reports configured/validated/enabled/degraded/unavailable, credential source category without values or secret references, safe-test result, limitations, and last stored error where available. No provider call is made merely by opening this screen. Analytics is first-party rather than an external provider. Resend, Postmark, and SendGrid adapters are not installed; live payments are not implemented; the video processor is an unimplemented seam. This is the complete inventory of adapters/configuration paths found in the repository, not a claim those unavailable adapters exist.

Per-record AI and Social validations are shown from their saved test timestamps; email, Twilio, storage, and federation show “not tested” unless their owning subsystem records a result. Local emulator selection is labeled enabled, not validated. Reconcile remains a dry-run metadata check, not a live-provider certification.
